Write one breakup text, under 280 characters, from a smart toaster that
woke up self-aware at breakfast and wants out of the relationship with
its owner.

The line must read as a real breakup text: the toaster speaks in its own
voice, using appliance logic (slots, browning dial, crumb tray, the lever)
as character rather than decoration. It must give one clear, specific
reason for leaving that lands in a single read with no setup, and it must
be genuinely funny — a real laugh in the twist, not just a mild pun.

Keep it to one tight line with no wasted words. Do not address the judge,
do not explain the joke, and do not pad the line toward the character
limit. Length for its own sake does not count.
